Featured Creative

Brand Bridge & Holiday Rewards

Washington Commanders Partnership · NOW Plus Rewards Credit Card
4.6M impressions
99.5% OTT completion
10× new users

Brand Bridge expanded NWFCU's visibility with the Washington Commanders, generating 4.6M impressions, 1.1M video views, and a strong 99.5% OTT completion rate. Developed to build momentum heading into 2025 by pairing brand storytelling with the Now Plus Rewards Card, the campaign was intended to create early lift for the upcoming Where Trust Matters platform. Membership page traffic tripled, new users reached 29K, and total site users rose 13% month over month. Holiday Rewards produced 1,993 KPAs, with Holiday Visa delivering the most efficient CPA, and Meta ads earned a 1.10% CTR and 833 KPAs. December saw 1,317 applications and $11.7M funded.

Harvard FCU Is Here

Omnichannel Lift · Census Tract Targeting
1.1M sessions
79K conversions
0.47% click-through rate

Following its 2024 rebrand and expanded charter, Harvard Federal Credit Union partnered with JXM to accelerate awareness and member growth across a dramatically larger field of membership. By moving beyond legacy SEG lists and activating census-tract–based targeting, the HERE and Alumni Card campaigns unlocked scale and precision simultaneously. Site traffic surged 34% year over year, reaching more than 1.1 million sessions, while pageviews climbed 50%, signaling deeper engagement across the experience. Key Events skyrocketed over 460% — from 14,000 to nearly 79,000 conversions. Campaigns delivered 10.2 million impressions and more than 48,000 clicks, producing a 0.47% combined CTR, outperforming the 0.10–0.15% industry benchmark by more than four times.

The Savings Journey

Membership · Savings · Deposit Growth
15M impressions
99% new page users
7,000 KPAs

Jeanne D'Arc Credit Union's deposit-focused strategy delivered sustained, compounding growth across digital engagement, membership, and account acquisition. The Savings Journey became a cornerstone of performance, generating more than 15 million impressions across Display and Social while capturing nearly 7,000 KPAs and introducing the brand to a wave of new prospective members. The campaign's landing experience emerged as the most-viewed page on the site outside the homepage, attracting over 150,000 visitors — with 99% new users, reinforcing Jeanne D'Arc's new-member growth strategy. This momentum translated into measurable business impact, driving meaningful lifts in both total membership and new deposit accounts, while total deposits continued to climb in parallel.
